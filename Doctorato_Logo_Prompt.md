# DOCTORATO — AI Logo Generation Prompt for NanoBanana

---

## PROMPT VERSION A — Primary (Detailed & Comprehensive)

**Prompt:**

Design a premium, modern, and minimal logo for **"Doctorato"** — a comprehensive SaaS medical clinic and healthcare management platform. The logo must convey trust, authority, innovation, and medical professionalism.

**Brand Name:** Doctorato
**Arabic Name:** دكتوراتو (for optional bilingual version)
**Industry:** Healthcare Technology / Medical SaaS / Clinic Management Software
**Target Audience:** Medical clinics, dental practices, healthcare centers, hospitals across the Middle East and internationally

**Design Requirements:**

1. **Wordmark + Icon Combination (Logo Lockup):**
   - Create a distinctive icon/symbol that can stand alone as an app icon or favicon
   - Pair it with a clean wordmark spelling "Doctorato"
   - The icon should be separable from the text for use on its own (app icons, favicons, watermarks)

2. **Icon Concept Directions (choose the strongest):**
   - A stylized letter "D" incorporating a subtle medical cross, stethoscope curve, or digital pulse line
   - An abstract shield or hexagonal shape symbolizing data protection and trust, with a medical element inside
   - A modern monogram combining "D" with a health/tech symbol (heartbeat line, DNA helix, or cross)
   - A minimalist tooth + medical cross fusion (representing the dental module as a key differentiator)
   - An elegant geometric "D" with a subtle gradient suggesting digital transformation in healthcare

3. **Typography:**
   - Use a modern geometric sans-serif font for "Doctorato" (similar to: Poppins Bold, Montserrat SemiBold, Proxima Nova, or custom lettering)
   - Clean, confident letterforms — not overly thin, not overly heavy
   - Optional: subtle customization on the "D" or "o" letters to make the wordmark distinctive
   - Letter spacing should feel open and premium (not cramped)

4. **Color Palette (STRICT):**
   - **Primary:** Deep Navy Blue `#1B4F72` — trust, authority, medical professionalism
   - **Secondary:** Elegant Gold `#C4A265` — premium, luxury, excellence
   - **Accent:** Bright Blue `#2E86C1` — technology, innovation, digital
   - The icon should primarily use Navy Blue with a Gold accent element
   - Avoid using more than 2-3 colors in the logo
   - Must work beautifully in single-color (all navy, all white, all black)

5. **Style Guidelines:**
   - Minimal and clean — no unnecessary ornaments, no clip-art style
   - Geometric and precise — balanced proportions, optical alignment
   - Premium feel — think Apple Health, Stripe, Linear, Notion level of design quality
   - Timeless — avoid trendy effects that will look dated in 2-3 years
   - No gradients in the primary version (flat color). Gradient version can be a secondary variant
   - Professional but approachable — not cold/corporate, not playful/childish
   - Should feel like a tech company logo, not a hospital logo

6. **Deliverables Needed:**
   - Primary logo (horizontal lockup: icon + wordmark)
   - Stacked version (icon on top, wordmark below)
   - Icon only (square format, for app icon / favicon)
   - Monochrome versions: all-white (for dark backgrounds), all-black, all-navy
   - Arabic version with "دكتوراتو" in Tajawal Bold font alongside the icon
   - Clear space and minimum size guidelines

7. **What to AVOID:**
   - Generic medical symbols (red cross, caduceus, syringe) used literally
   - Overly complex illustrations or detailed imagery
   - Rounded/bubbly/childish aesthetics
   - Thin hairline fonts or ultra-light weights
   - More than 3 colors
   - Clip-art or stock icon style
   - Resemblance to existing medical software logos (eClinicalWorks, Epic, Cerner, Vezeeta)

8. **Context for the Designer:**
   Doctorato is a powerful, feature-rich platform with 6 independent portals, 800+ features, 346 interactive screens, and modules for dental care, CRM, HR, insurance, and inventory. The logo needs to represent this scale and sophistication while remaining elegantly simple. It will appear on: the web application navbar, marketing website, mobile app, PDF reports, invoices, business cards, presentation decks, and email signatures. It must work at sizes from 16x16px favicon to large billboard format.
