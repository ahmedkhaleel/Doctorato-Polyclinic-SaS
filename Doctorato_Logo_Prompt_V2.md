# DOCTORATO — Logo Prompt V2 (Simple & Attractive)

---

## PROMPT 1 — The Stethoscope "D" (Recommended)

Design a simple, elegant logo for **"Doctorato"** — a medical clinic management platform.

**Concept:** The letter **"D"** is cleverly formed from a **stethoscope silhouette** — the tube curves to create the "D" shape, and the chest piece sits naturally at the base. The design is ultra-minimal, using only clean lines with no unnecessary details. Next to the icon, the word "Doctorato" is written in a modern, bold sans-serif font.

**Colors:** Navy blue `#1B4F72` for the icon and text. A single golden dot `#C4A265` replaces the dot above the "o" or acts as the stethoscope earpiece — one small gold accent that makes the whole logo pop.

**Style:** Flat, single-weight line art. No gradients, no shadows. Think of how a luxury medical brand would look — clean like Apple, confident like Stripe. Must work at 16px favicon size and on business cards.

## PROMPT 2 — The Smart Cross

Design a minimal logo for **"Doctorato"** — a healthcare SaaS platform.

**Concept:** A modern **medical plus/cross sign (+)** with a subtle twist — the four arms of the cross are made of **rounded rectangles with slightly different lengths**, creating a sense of motion and digital intelligence, like a loading or processing symbol. Alternatively, one arm of the cross transitions into a **digital pulse/heartbeat line**. The cross sits inside a **soft rounded square** (like an app icon shape). Next to it, "Doctorato" in clean geometric typography.

**Colors:** The cross icon in navy `#1B4F72`. The pulse line or one arm accent in gold `#C4A265`. White background. The rounded square border can be a subtle light blue `#EBF5FB`.

**Style:** Geometric, precise, app-icon ready. Inspired by modern health-tech startups. No vintage, no ornamental elements.

## PROMPT 3 — The Crown Doctor

Design a distinctive logo for **"Doctorato"** — a premium medical management system.

**Concept:** The word "Doctorato" contains "Doctor" + the suffix evokes prestige (like "doctorate"). The icon is a **minimal crown** where the three crown points are subtly shaped like **medical crosses or heartbeat peaks**. This communicates both medical authority and premium quality. The crown is small, elegant — not royal or heavy, more like a subtle stamp of excellence. It can sit above the "D" in Doctorato or standalone.

**Colors:** Navy blue `#1B4F72` crown and text. The crown tips or inner detail in gold `#C4A265`. Clean, two-color only.

**Style:** Luxury-minimal. Think of how premium watch brands or 5-star hotel logos look — understated, confident, memorable. The crown should be geometric and modern, NOT ornate or medieval.

## PROMPT 4 — The Shield of Trust

Design a professional logo for **"Doctorato"** — a secure medical data management platform.

**Concept:** A **minimalist shield** outline — soft rounded corners, modern proportions (not medieval). Inside the shield, a simple **"D"** letterform with a small **medical cross** integrated into the negative space of the D. The shield represents data security and patient trust. The overall shape works perfectly as a square app icon. "Doctorato" written beside it in a confident, medium-weight sans-serif.

**Colors:** Shield outline and "D" in navy `#1B4F72`. The small medical cross accent in gold `#C4A265`. Optional: very subtle gradient from navy to bright blue `#2E86C1` on the shield (secondary version only).

**Style:** Tech-forward, trustworthy. Similar to security/fintech brand aesthetics (like 1Password, NordVPN) but medical. Clean lines, no texture.

## PROMPT 5 — The Dot System (Ultra-Minimal)

Design an ultra-simple logo for **"Doctorato"**.

**Concept:** Pure wordmark — the word **"Doctorato"** in a custom geometric sans-serif font (similar to Poppins or Cerebri Sans). The only distinctive element: the **dot on the letter "o"** (the last "o") is replaced with a **golden circle** — slightly larger than a normal dot, perfectly round, in gold `#C4A265`. This single golden dot becomes the entire brand mark. When used as a favicon or app icon, it's simply a navy `#1B4F72` circle containing that golden dot, or the letter "D" with the golden dot beside it.

**Colors:** All text in navy `#1B4F72`. One golden dot `#C4A265`. Nothing else. Two colors total.

**Style:** Extremely minimal. The kind of logo that looks like it took 5 minutes but actually required 500 iterations to get perfect. Every proportion and spacing is intentional. Inspired by: Google's simplicity, Notion's restraint, Medium's elegance.

## PROMPT 6 — The Heartbeat D (Dynamic)

Design a modern, energetic logo for **"Doctorato"**.

**Concept:** The letter **"D"** is constructed with a clean vertical stroke on the left, but instead of a normal curve on the right side, the curve is replaced by a **single heartbeat/ECG pulse line** (one peak — up, sharp point, down) that flows from top to bottom, naturally forming the "D" shape. It's one continuous line — simple, recognizable, and uniquely medical. The wordmark "Doctorato" follows in matching weight.

**Colors:** The D-icon and wordmark in navy `#1B4F72`. The peak of the heartbeat pulse highlighted in gold `#C4A265` — just the tip, like a spark of life. Two colors, maximum impact.

**Style:** Dynamic but controlled. The heartbeat adds energy without making it feel clinical. Suitable for a tech company that happens to serve healthcare — not a hospital logo. Must reproduce cleanly in single color and at small sizes.

---

## PROMPT 7 — Arabic-English Bilingual (Elegant)

Design a bilingual logo for **"Doctorato / دكتوراتو"**.

**Concept:** A centered layout. The icon (any of the above concepts — stethoscope D, shield, crown, or heartbeat D) sits in the center. Below it, **"Doctorato"** in English (Poppins SemiBold). Below that, **"دكتوراتو"** in Arabic (Tajawal Bold) at a slightly smaller size. Both lines are center-aligned. The Arabic text should feel like a natural part of the design, not an afterthought. Alternatively: English and Arabic side by side, separated by the icon or a thin gold line.

**Colors:** Same navy + gold palette. The Arabic text can be in a slightly lighter navy or in the bright blue `#2E86C1` to create visual hierarchy.

**Style:** The bilingual version must feel balanced — Arabic calligraphic weight should visually match English geometric weight. Test both horizontal and stacked layouts.

## GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS FOR ALL VERSIONS

**Brand:** Doctorato (دكتوراتو)
**Industry:** Medical Clinic Management SaaS
**Company:** Markeza Group (مجموعة ماركيزا للتقنية)

**Color Codes:**
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Deep Navy | `#1B4F72` | Primary — icon, text |
| Elegant Gold | `#C4A265` | Accent — one highlight element |
| Bright Blue | `#2E86C1` | Secondary — optional gradient or bilingual |
| Dark BG | `#0D2B45` | For reversed/white logo on dark |

**Fonts:**
- English: Poppins SemiBold or Montserrat SemiBold
- Arabic: Tajawal Bold

**Deliverables per concept:**
- Horizontal lockup (icon + wordmark)
- Stacked version (icon above wordmark)
- Icon only (app icon / favicon — square)
- White version (for dark backgrounds)
- Black version (for print)
- Arabic bilingual version

**Must work on:**
- White background, dark navy background, light blue `#EBF5FB` background
- Sizes: 16x16, 32x32, 64x64, 128x128, 256x256, 512x512, and full-size print

**Avoid:**
- Complex illustrations or gradients in primary version
- Generic medical clipart (syringes, pills, hospital buildings)
- More than 2 colors in any single version
- Thin/light font weights
- Anything that looks like an existing medical app logo
